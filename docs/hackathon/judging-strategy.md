# Judging Strategy — Anveshan

**Review checkpoint:** 2026-10-03

## Official weighting

| Criterion | Weight | Proof required |
|---|---:|---|
| Technical Merit & Gen AI Implementation | 40% | AI is essential, technically meaningful, functional and scalable |
| Problem Alignment & Impact | 25% | A real transmission problem with measurable value |
| Innovation & Creativity | 25% | Differentiation survives direct competitor comparison |
| User Experience & Solution Design | 10% | The loop is intuitive and cohesive |

## Technical Merit — 40%

Show multimodal expert capture, video/audio understanding, source-grounded retrieval, structured knowledge, learner-attempt analysis, criterion-level evaluation, justified agent/tool orchestration, evaluation traces and Cloud Run deployment.

The architecture should make clear why AI is necessary rather than decorative.

## Problem Alignment & Impact — 25%

Problem:

> Valuable expertise can fail to reach the next learner because it is difficult to capture, contextualize, teach and verify.

The strongest impact evidence is baseline → intervention → retry → measured change → expert review. Do not substitute speculative population numbers for demonstrated value.

## Innovation & Creativity — 25%

Do not claim AI interviews, tacit extraction, RAG, knowledge graphs or video assessment as individually novel.

Show the integrated transmission event:

**expert source → grounded knowledge → teaching → learner attempt → verification → improvement → lineage**

## UX — 10%

The judge should understand:

**EXPERT SHOWS → ANVESHAN GROUNDS → LEARNER TRIES → ANVESHAN VERIFIES → LEARNER IMPROVES → TRANSMISSION RECORDED**

## Judge evidence matrix

| Question | Visible proof |
|---|---|
| Where did the knowledge come from? | Original source + timestamp |
| Is AI hallucinating? | Evidence link + uncertainty |
| What if experts disagree? | Variant/contradiction view |
| Why not an AI tutor? | Expert-source-to-learner trace |
| Why not an archive? | Learner attempt + improvement |
| Why is it technically difficult? | Multimodal extraction + evaluation + lineage |
| Does it work? | Deployed end-to-end prototype |

See docs/hackathon/judge-qa.md for the full question set.

## Principle

The prototype should do the explaining. Architecture narration must not substitute for working behavior.
