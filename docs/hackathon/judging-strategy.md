# Judging Strategy — Anveshan

## Official Evaluation Weights Recorded

| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Technical Merit & GenAI Implementation | 40% |
| Problem Alignment & Impact | 25% |
| Innovation & Creativity | 25% |
| User Experience & Solution Design | 10% |

## 1. Technical Merit & GenAI Implementation — 40%

The implementation should visibly demonstrate meaningful AI work.

Potential evidence:

- Gemini multimodal understanding
- speech/transcription
- video understanding
- multimodal retrieval
- semantic embeddings
- structured knowledge extraction
- evidence grounding
- agent orchestration
- learner-attempt analysis
- evaluation
- Cloud Run deployment
- observability
- secure access.

Avoid shallow "prompt wrapper" architecture.

## 2. Problem Alignment & Impact — 25%

The problem should be understandable:

> Valuable human expertise can disappear or become inaccessible when it is not successfully transmitted.

The demo should make the human consequence visible.

Potential impact dimensions:

- intergenerational knowledge
- community knowledge
- skilled learning
- local expertise
- workforce continuity
- sustainability
- social impact.

Do not inflate impact claims beyond the demonstrated evidence.

## 3. Innovation & Creativity — 25%

The innovation hypothesis is the combination of:

- source-grounded multimodal knowledge extraction
- knowledge graph
- adaptive apprenticeship
- observable learner practice
- evidence-based verification
- transmission measurement
- lineage/evolution.

The product should make clear why it is more than:

- an archive
- RAG
- a tutor
- an interview bot
- a video summarizer.

## 4. User Experience & Solution Design — 10%

The experience should be simple:

**Expert → Demonstrate → AI understands → Learner learns → Learner attempts → AI verifies → Knowledge continues.**

Avoid making judges navigate a large administrative product.

## Judge-Facing Questions to Preempt

### Where did this knowledge come from?

Answer through source media and timestamps.

### How do you know the AI is not hallucinating?

Show evidence grounding and uncertainty.

### What happens when experts disagree?

Show variants and contradiction handling.

### What makes this different from an AI tutor?

The learner is learning from authentic expert evidence, the system tracks the source, and the learner's attempt is evaluated against that knowledge.

### What makes this different from a knowledge archive?

The system measures actual transmission.

### What makes this different from industrial knowledge-transfer products?

The initial product is a general human-knowledge transmission architecture and its differentiator is the source-grounded transmission/verification/lineage loop, not retirement capture alone.

### Why is this technically difficult?

Because the system must connect multimodal source understanding, provenance, graph representation, adaptive teaching, learner evidence, evaluation, and lineage.

## Demonstration Principle

Show the system performing the hard thing.

Do not spend most of the demo describing architecture.
