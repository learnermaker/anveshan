**One line:**
Anveshan passes on skills of the physical world from one person to another: learned from a real demonstration, practised with real hands, and checked against a photo, video or recording of the learner's real attempt.

---

**25-word description:**
Anveshan captures a real skill demonstration, builds a grounded teaching model, observes what you physically do, and shows measured improvement across retries.

---

**50-word description:**
Most skills of the physical world — folding, drawing, drumming, repairing — are hard to pass on because text and video can show but never look back at what your hands did. Anveshan captures a real demonstration, structures what the expert knows, watches your attempt, and gives feedback grounded in the source.

---

**150-word description:**
Skills of the physical world leave the world when their practitioners do. Text and video courses can show a skill, but they cannot see what your hands did or tell you exactly where you went off track.

Anveshan closes that gap. A creator uploads a real demonstration — a photo, video or audio recording. The system extracts the key steps, a rubric and evidence grounded in the source. When a learner submits their attempt, deterministic checks compare it to the goal model: no model decides whether a criterion passes or fails. The first place the attempt diverged is found, and Gemini words a tip that points back to the source moment. On the retry, the same checks run and the change is measured.

The result is not a better tutor. It is a measurable transmission event: one person's knowledge, moved to another, with the evidence preserved.

---

**10-word tagline:**
The skill transfers. The evidence stays.

---

**Why it matters:**

- **Heritage.** Craft knowledge, folk art and traditional practices that live in practitioners' hands — not in any book — can now be structured, taught and verified before those practitioners are gone.

- **Livelihoods.** Skilled trades and artisanal knowledge are marketable. A creator who can publish a verifiable skill model has a product, a record of learner outcomes and a basis for recognition or income.

- **Access.** Anyone with a phone can practise a bounded task and receive feedback that points to the exact moment in the real demonstration. No studio, no tutor, no special equipment.
