## Research rule

A source can do one of three jobs:

1. **Market evidence** — competitor capability.
2. **Technical evidence** — current platform/model capability.
3. **Domain/ethical evidence** — learning, heritage, governance or methodological evidence.

Time-sensitive claims must be rechecked before submission. Research is not product validation.
