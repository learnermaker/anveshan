# Rejected and Deferred Directions

This document records directions that were explored but are not currently the primary product direction.

## 1. Generic Family Memoir App

Why it was attractive:
- emotional
- easy to explain
- accessible
- strong family use case.

Why not primary:
- crowded by Storyworth, Remento, FamilySearch and adjacent products.
- AI interviewing and memoir generation are insufficient novelty.

Status: **REJECTED AS PRIMARY WEDGE; POSSIBLE FUTURE APPLICATION**

## 2. AI Grandparent Interviewer

Why attractive:
- clear demo
- human emotion
- conversational AI.

Why not primary:
- AI interview itself is commoditized.
- follow-up questions are not sufficient moat.
- still primarily a memory/story product.

Status: **REJECTED AS PRODUCT DEFINITION; CAPTURE FEATURE MAY SURVIVE**

## 3. Family Tree + AI

Why attractive:
- natural family-history extension.

Why not primary:
- genealogy is mature.
- does not address transmission of embodied expertise.

Status: **REJECTED AS PRIMARY PRODUCT**

## 4. Generic Cultural Archive

Why attractive:
- social impact
- heritage preservation
- multimodal data.

Why not primary:
- active competitors and government/community initiatives.
- archive-only model risks static preservation rather than living transmission.

Status: **REJECTED AS PRIMARY PRODUCT; CULTURAL DOMAIN MAY BE MVP WEDGE**

## 5. Generic Industrial Tacit-Knowledge Platform

Why attractive:
- obvious ROI
- strong enterprise problem
- retirement risk.

Why not primary:
- SightCall, Poka, Dozuki, DeepHow, Mission Control and related categories already address major parts of this space.

Status: **REJECTED AS PRIMARY HACKATHON WEDGE; FUTURE EXPANSION**

## 6. Generic AI Apprenticeship

Why attractive:
- technically interesting
- easy to demonstrate.

Why not primary:
- AI tutoring and apprenticeship research/products already exist.
- without provenance and authentic source knowledge it becomes a generic tutor.

Status: **REJECTED AS COMPLETE PRODUCT; TRANSMISSION ENGINE SURVIVES**

## 7. Generic RAG over Human Stories

Why attractive:
- technically straightforward.

Why not primary:
- does not make knowledge transmissible.
- weak distinction from document search.

Status: **REJECTED**

## 8. AI Avatar of an Elder

Why attractive:
- emotionally compelling demo.

Why not primary:
- authenticity concerns.
- risk of fabricated authority.
- source integrity becomes ambiguous.

Status: **REJECTED**

## 9. Healthcare Knowledge Transfer

Why attractive:
- high impact.

Why deferred:
- high-stakes domain.
- evaluation and safety requirements are much harder.
- poor choice for first proof unless a tightly bounded non-clinical use case emerges.

Status: **DEFERRED**

## 10. Emergency / Disaster Expert System

Why attractive:
- strong social impact.
- local knowledge can matter.

Why deferred:
- high-stakes.
- validation burden.
- difficult to demonstrate safely.

Status: **DEFERRED**

## 11. Knowledge Marketplace as MVP

Why attractive:
- potential long-term business model.

Why deferred:
- marketplace dynamics are orthogonal to proving transmission.
- adds identity, payments, trust, moderation, scheduling, and discovery complexity.

Status: **FUTURE**

## 12. Universal Knowledge Platform from Day One

Why attractive:
- broad vision.

Why rejected:
- impossible to validate quickly.
- creates ontology and UX complexity.
- weak demo focus.

Status: **REJECTED FOR MVP**

## 13. Massive Multi-Agent Swarm

Why attractive:
- visually impressive.
- agentic hackathon narrative.

Why rejected:
- agent count is not technical merit.
- increases failure modes.
- makes evaluation difficult.

Status: **REJECTED AS ARCHITECTURAL GOAL**

## 14. Feature-Maximal MVP

Why attractive:
- broad product impression.

Why rejected:
- prevents proving the core loop.
- creates shallow implementation.

Status: **REJECTED**

## 15. Core Product Naming as Katha

Why attractive:
- culturally resonant.
- strong association with stories.

Why deferred:
- too strongly associated with stories, memories, and oral history.
- product thesis has broadened.

Status: **DEFERRED; PRODUCT NAME TBD**

## 16. Current Retained Direction

The surviving direction is:

**Source-grounded multimodal transmission of living, embodied human knowledge.**

The initial proof should be narrow enough to prove the loop, while the architecture preserves the ability to expand.
