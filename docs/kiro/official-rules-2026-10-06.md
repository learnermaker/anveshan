## Submission Requirements

### What to build
> "A working prototype that addresses your chosen problem statement. The goal is a functional solution that demonstrates a clear idea in action—not just a pitch deck or a mockup."

Required: leverage **Google AI models** (Gemini or Gemma) or be built using **agentic platforms** (Agent Platform, Antigravity, or AI Studio), and **deployed on Google Cloud via Cloud Run or Firebase**.

### What to submit (verbatim where short)

1. **Clear Proposal**
   > "Submit a compelling vision for your AI solution, highlighting its positive impact and alignment with one of the problem statements in a **deck/PPT converted into a PDF**. Clearly articulate the problem your solution addresses and how it will benefit the community. Demonstrate the feasibility of your project and its potential for scalability."

2. **Functional Prototype**
   > "Build a working prototype using Google Cloud's AI tools & deployed on **Cloud Run/GCP/Firebase**. Ensure the prototype effectively demonstrates your solution's capabilities and benefits."

3. **Comprehensive Documentation**
   > "Include a link to a **public YouTube or Vimeo video or public Google Drive link (3 minutes)** showcasing your AI creation in action."

4. **Category Specification**
   > "Clearly identify the problem statement you are submitting for (Healthcare, Education, Sustainability, Accessibility, Social Good)."
   **[NOTE: this parenthetical list does not match the six official theme names — it appears to be stale template text. Use the official theme name "Sustainability & Social Impact" when submitting.]**

## Judging Criteria

The themes page lists four weighted groups. The exact percentages were not printed on the page in the version fetched; the weights below come from `docs/hackathon/ai-builder-cup.md` (which recorded them as of 2026-10-03) and are consistent with the criteria descriptions.

| Criterion group | Weight | Sub-criteria (verbatim from official page) |
|---|---|---|
| Technical Merit & Gen AI Implementation | 40% | "Meaningful use of Gen AI: How effectively and meaningfully has Generative AI been incorporated into the solution? Does the use of Gen AI contribute directly to solving the problem or enhancing the overall solution?" / "Technical implementation: Is the solution functional, technically sound, and appropriately implemented?" / "Scalability & sustainability: Does the solution demonstrate potential for wider adoption, scalability, and future development beyond the prototype stage?" |
| Problem Alignment & Impact | 25% | "Problem alignment: How effectively does the solution address the chosen problem statement, challenge objective, or theme?" / "Potential impact: Does the solution demonstrate clear and meaningful value for its intended users, organization, industry, community, or other relevant stakeholders?" |
| Innovation & Creativity | 25% | "Originality & differentiation: Does the solution demonstrate a fresh perspective, novel approach, or meaningful differentiation from existing solutions?" / "Innovation in the use of Gen AI: Does the solution demonstrate a creative or innovative application of Generative AI beyond a basic or superficial implementation?" |
| User Experience & Solution Design | 10% | "Usability & accessibility: Is the solution intuitive, user-friendly, and reasonably accessible to its intended users?" / "Experience & integration: Do the various components, including the Gen AI capabilities, work together cohesively to deliver a smooth and meaningful user experience?" |

## Team Rules

From the main page:
- **Team size:** 2–4 members. You may register solo and join a team later, but teams must be 2–4 members by October 11, 2026.
- If shortlisted for the finale, **only 2 members travel to Singapore** (sponsored travel).
- **Age:** 21+ only.
- **Region:** JAPAC.
- **Working professionals only.** > "This edition is open to working professionals only. Students are not eligible to participate. Eligibility may be verified at any point, and any team or participant found to include a student will be disqualified, including after shortlisting or selection for the finale."
