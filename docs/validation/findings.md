## Newly Documented Research Hypotheses

### Goal-conditioned transmission

A desired visual outcome may be sufficient to initiate a transmission workflow when the capability can be represented through observable target structure and plausible intermediate states.

**Current status:** unvalidated hypothesis.

Kolam is the first candidate because research supports its treatment as a structured geometric practice. However, a final Kolam image does not establish the original artist's construction procedure, intent, or cultural meaning. Any generated construction path must therefore be labeled as inferred/plausible unless corroborated by a source demonstration.

### Adaptive source-to-workspace composition

Anveshan may need to choose a transmission mode based on source type:

- demonstration → forward transmission;
- desired state → goal-conditioned reconstruction;
- learner attempt → corrective transmission.

**Current status:** unvalidated product/GenUI hypothesis.
